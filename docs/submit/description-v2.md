# Landed（Landed）— 提交页描述（更新版）

## 一句话（Tagline）
跨境找人、跨境接单的链上托管平台：先谈妥，再锁钱，干完到账。
Hire or freelance across borders. Agree first, lock funds on Avalanche, get paid on delivery.

## 简介（中文）
找人的怕付了钱拿不到活，接单的怕干完活拿不到钱。Landed 同时服务两边：
需求方发带预算的需求，技能方挂出技能、参考价和作品集（图片、视频、社媒链接）。
价格和需求都能先谈，出价、还价像聊天一样来回，谈妥后客户一键把预算以 USDC 锁进 Avalanche 合约。
按里程碑交付、验收、放款；客户超时不回应，接单人到期自行领取；有争议可冻结剩余款项交仲裁。
完成记录写在链上，信用跟着钱包地址走。用钱包登录，无需注册密码，不会被封号。0 平台抽成，几秒到账。

## Description (English)
Clients worry about paying and getting nothing; freelancers worry about working and not getting paid. Landed serves both sides.
Clients post jobs with a budget; freelancers list skills with a starting price and a portfolio (images, videos, social links).
Price and scope are negotiable: offers and counter-offers go back and forth like a chat, and once both agree the client locks the budget in USDC on an Avalanche contract.
Work is delivered and paid milestone by milestone. If the client goes silent, the freelancer claims the payment after the review window; disputes freeze the remainder for arbitration.
Completed work builds on-chain reputation tied to the wallet. Log in with a wallet: no password, no account freezes. Zero platform fee, settled in seconds.

## 功能列表 / Features
- 需求广场 Job board（预算已锁定）/ 技能广场 Skills board（参考价 + 作品集）
- 议价 Negotiation：申请附报价；技能出价、还价、同意，谈妥后一键锁钱
- 里程碑托管、超时自动领取、争议仲裁 Milestone escrow, timeout claim, arbitration
- 私单直发 Direct escrow links
- 个人页 Profile：资料编辑、我发的需求 / 我接的单 / 我的技能 / 议价记录、链上信用
- 钱包登录 / 注册 Wallet log-in with first-time onboarding
- 中文 / English / Español / 日本語，手机可用
